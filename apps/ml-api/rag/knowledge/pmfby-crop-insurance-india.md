# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) crop insurance in India

## What PMFBY is

PMFBY is the national crop insurance scheme launched in 2016. It covers yield losses from natural calamities, pests and diseases for notified crops in notified areas. In Odisha, it is widely used for kharif paddy and rabi crops.

## Farmer premium

Farmers pay a small share; central and state governments pay the rest.

| Crop type | Farmer premium (share of sum insured) |
|---|---|
| Kharif food and oilseed crops | 2% |
| Rabi food and oilseed crops | 1.5% |
| Annual commercial and horticultural crops | 5% |

## What is covered

1. **Prevented sowing:** if most of the notified area could not be sown because of rain deficit or adverse weather.
2. **Standing crop losses** from drought, flood, inundation, pests, diseases, cyclone and similar risks, assessed through crop-cutting experiments.
3. **Localised calamities:** damage to individual farms from hailstorm, landslide, **inundation**, cloudburst and natural fire, assessed farm by farm.
4. **Post-harvest losses:** damage from cyclone, cyclonic rain and unseasonal rain to crops kept in "cut and spread" condition in the field, up to **14 days after harvest**.
5. **Mid-season adversity:** on-account payments where a notified area suffers severe losses.

## Enrolment

- Enrolment was compulsory for loanee farmers until 2020; it is now voluntary for all farmers. Loanee farmers can opt out by informing their bank.
- Non-loanee farmers enrol through a bank branch, Common Service Centre (CSC), authorised insurance agent, or the national crop insurance portal.
- The kharif enrolment deadline is usually around **31 July** and the rabi deadline around **31 December**; states can notify different dates. Check each season.

### Documents
- Aadhaar card
- Bank passbook (account linked to Aadhaar)
- Land record (Record of Rights) or tenancy or sharecropping agreement as allowed by the state
- Sowing declaration or certificate

## Reporting a loss — act within 72 hours

For localised calamities such as **inundation** and for post-harvest losses:

1. **Report within 72 hours** of the event to the insurance company, the bank, the local agriculture office (Block Agriculture Officer or Assistant Agriculture Officer), or through the official crop insurance app or the national crop insurance helpline.
2. Give: name, crop, survey or plot number, area affected, date and cause of loss.
3. Get a **docket or complaint number** and keep it.
4. Take **dated, geo-tagged photos** of the damaged field.
5. Do not destroy the damaged crop until the joint assessment team (insurer and agriculture department) inspects it, normally within the time set by the guidelines.

## Tips for Odisha coastal farmers

- Enrol kharif paddy before the deadline; cyclones and floods peak in October–November.
- Keep copies of the enrolment receipt and bank premium deduction.
- After a cyclone, report both standing crop damage and harvested crop lying in the field.
- Check claim status using your application number on the portal or at the bank.
- State input subsidy for crop loss of 33% or more after declared disasters is separate from PMFBY; you can receive both if eligible.
