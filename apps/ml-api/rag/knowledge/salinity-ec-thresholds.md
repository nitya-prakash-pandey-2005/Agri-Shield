# Salinity EC thresholds for crops and water

## ECe and ECw

Salinity is measured as electrical conductivity (EC) in deciSiemens per metre (dS/m). 1 dS/m equals 1 mS/cm and roughly 640 mg/L (about 0.64 g/L or 0.64 ppt) of dissolved salt.

- **ECe** is the EC of the soil saturation extract. Crop thresholds in FAO tables use ECe.
- **ECw** is the EC of irrigation water. As a rough rule, if irrigation continues, soil ECe ends up about 1.5 times ECw.
- **EC 1:5** is from a simple soil:water mix used with handheld meters; it must be converted to ECe (see the soil testing guidance).

## Soil salinity classes (ECe)

| ECe (dS/m) | Class | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| below 2 | Non-saline | Safe for almost all crops |
| 2–4 | Slightly saline | Sensitive crops (beans, onion, maize) lose yield |
| 4–8 | Moderately saline | Many crops lose yield; grow tolerant crops and varieties |
| 8–16 | Strongly saline | Only tolerant crops (barley, cotton, sugar beet, salt-tolerant rice at the lower end) |
| above 16 | Very strongly saline | Very few crops survive |

## Crop thresholds (Maas–Hoffman, FAO 29)

Threshold is the ECe at which yield starts to fall. Slope is the yield loss for each 1 dS/m above the threshold.

| Crop | Threshold ECe (dS/m) | Loss per dS/m above |
|---|---|---|
| Rice (paddy) | 3.0 | about 12% |
| Wheat | 6.0 | about 7% |
| Barley | 8.0 | about 5% |
| Cotton | 7.7 | about 5% |
| Sorghum | 6.8 | about 16% |
| Sugar beet | 7.0 | about 6% |
| Soybean | 5.0 | about 20% |
| Groundnut | 3.2 | about 29% |
| Tomato | 2.5 | about 10% |
| Cucumber | 2.5 | about 13% |
| Spinach | 2.0 | about 8% |
| Cabbage | 1.8 | about 10% |
| Maize | 1.7 | about 12% |
| Sugarcane | 1.7 | about 6% |
| Potato | 1.7 | about 12% |
| Lettuce | 1.3 | about 13% |
| Onion | 1.2 | about 16% |
| Beans | 1.0 | about 19% |

**Worked example for rice:** at ECe 5.0 dS/m, rice is 2 dS/m above the threshold, so expected yield loss is about 2 x 12% = 24%. At about ECe 7 dS/m, yield is roughly halved.

## Irrigation water quality (FAO 29)

| ECw (dS/m) | Restriction on use |
|---|---|
| below 0.7 | None |
| 0.7–3.0 | Slight to moderate |
| above 3.0 | Severe |

For rice, water up to about ECw 2 dS/m generally gives full yield; above that, yield falls. Salt-tolerant rice varieties can manage somewhat higher levels.

## Sensitive growth stages

- Rice is most sensitive at the **seedling stage** and from **panicle initiation to flowering**. Salinity at flowering causes empty grains.
- Most vegetables are sensitive at germination and early seedling stage.

## Measuring EC with a handheld meter

1. Calibrate the meter with its standard solution before each season.
2. For water, dip the probe in canal, pond or field water, wait until the reading is stable, and note the value and time (tide stage matters).
3. For soil, mix 1 part dry soil with 5 parts clean water, shake for a few minutes, let settle and read.
4. Rinse the probe with clean water after each reading.

## Actions by salinity level

- **Below 2 dS/m:** normal cropping.
- **2–4 dS/m:** use tolerant varieties, **flush** with fresh water, avoid irrigating with saline water at flowering.
- **4–8 dS/m:** grow salt-tolerant rice or tolerant dry-season crops (barley, sunflower, cotton, some wheat); leach before planting.
- **Above 8 dS/m:** leave fallow, leach with rain, or consider aquaculture or rice–shrimp systems.
