# Crop insurance options in Bangladesh, Vietnam and Indonesia

## Bangladesh

Bangladesh does not yet have a nationwide crop insurance scheme for smallholders. Options available include:

- **Weather index-based crop insurance** piloted by the state-owned **Sadharan Bima Corporation (SBC)** with Asian Development Bank support in selected districts. Payouts are triggered when rainfall or other weather measurements at a reference station cross set levels, not by field inspection.
- **Pilots by private insurers, NGOs and microfinance institutions**, sometimes bundled with loans, covering flood or livestock.
- **Livestock insurance** through some NGOs and cooperatives.

After disasters, the government provides **agricultural rehabilitation support** (free seed, fertilizer, cash) through the Department of Agricultural Extension (DAE). To access it:
1. Keep your **agricultural input assistance card (farmer card)** and National ID ready.
2. Report crop damage to the Sub-Assistant Agriculture Officer (SAAO) or Upazila Agriculture Office.
3. Ensure you are listed in the union-level damage list.

## Vietnam

Vietnam supports agricultural insurance under **Decree 58/2018/ND-CP** on agricultural insurance support policy. The government pays a large share of the premium for eligible farmers, with the highest support for poor and near-poor households and lower support for other smallholders, for insured rice, livestock and aquaculture in provinces selected under the programme. Support levels and eligible provinces are set by government decisions and change over time.

How to take part:
1. Ask the commune People's Committee or agricultural extension station whether your commune is in the insurance support programme.
2. Enrol through the participating insurance company before the crop season.
3. Keep the contract, receipts and planting records.
4. When a loss occurs, **notify the insurer and the commune authorities quickly**, usually within a few days, and before clearing or replanting.

## Indonesia — AUTP (Asuransi Usaha Tani Padi)

AUTP is the national rice crop insurance programme run by the Ministry of Agriculture (Kementan) with the state insurer (Jasindo) and partners.

- Covers rice against **flood, drought, pests and plant diseases**.
- The government subsidises most of the premium (about 80%); the farmer pays the remaining share, a small amount per hectare per season.
- A claim is paid when damage reaches about **75% or more** of the insured area (intensity and area), assessed by field officers.
- Farmers must be members of a farmer group (kelompok tani).

How to enrol and claim:
1. Register through your **kelompok tani** with help from the field extension officer (**PPL**) before or within the early weeks after planting.
2. Pay the farmer share and keep the proof.
3. If damage occurs, **report to the PPL and insurance officer promptly** (within about 7 days is commonly required).
4. The PPL and pest observer (POPT) inspect and verify damage.
5. Keep photos and group records.

Livestock insurance for cattle (AUTS) is also available in Indonesia.

## General documentation tips for all countries

1. **Record planting details:** variety, area, planting date, fertilizer and seed receipts.
2. **Take dated photos** before, during and after the flood or storm, showing water depth and damage.
3. **Report early** to the extension officer and insurer; late reports are often rejected.
4. **Do not replant or clear** the whole field before inspection; leave a sample area if replanting cannot wait.
5. Keep copies of every form and a record of whom you spoke to and when.
6. Check whether your loan, cooperative or farmer group already includes insurance.
